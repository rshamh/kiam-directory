Non-field errors sit in a role="alert" so a rate-limit refusal is announced rather than only appearing (WCAG 4.1.3) — the same pattern as the sign-in form. `novalidate` hands validation to the server. Browser bubbles disappear on their own, are not reliably announced, and cannot say "we couldn't find that listing", which is the error people will actually hit here. The honeypot is .sr-only rather than display:none — a display:none field is skipped by some bots, which is exactly backwards. {% endcomment %}
