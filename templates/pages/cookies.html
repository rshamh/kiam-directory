What this site sets today

Reading this directory sets nothing that tracks you. There are no advertising cookies, no third-party tags, and no cross-site identifiers.

Cookies set by this site
CookieWhat it doesWhen it is set
Display preferences Remembers your text size, contrast, motion and typeface choices from the accessibility panel. Only if you change one of those settings.
Sign-in session Keeps you signed in. Only if you sign in — practitioners and staff.
Form security Protects forms against being submitted from another site on your behalf. When a page with a form is shown.

Analytics

Listings show their owner how many times their page was viewed and their contact details revealed. Those are daily totals held on the listing, not records about you: nothing identifies a visitor, and nothing follows one between pages or between sites.

Turning things off

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking them will not stop you reading listings; it will mean your display preferences are forgotten between visits, and that signing in stops working.
