What a listing is

A page of your own on a public directory, with your profession, specialities, approaches, client groups, languages, locations, fees and availability, and your own contact details behind a click. People find you and contact you directly.

What a listing is not

  • It is not a referral stream from Kiam Clinic. Kiam does not match or triage clients to practitioners, and a listing does not put you in a queue for anything.
  • It is not a booking system. Enquiries reach you directly. Kiam relays no messages and holds no calendar.
  • It is not employment, association or supervision. You remain independent — your practice, your indemnity, your regulator. Every listing says so on the page.
  • Kiam takes no commission. Your fees are yours and are paid to you directly by your clients.

What you will need

  • Photo identification.
  • Current registration with your regulator or professional body, with the number and a link to your public register entry.
  • Proof of your qualifications.
  • Current professional indemnity insurance.
  • Your ICO registration.
  • An enhanced DBS certificate, if you want to be listed as working with under-18s. Without it a listing can still go live, but for adult work only — see how verification works.

Some professional titles are protected or constrained. Where yours is, the listing cannot be published until a registration with the relevant body has been verified — there is no route around that step.

How it works

  1. You are invited, and set up an account. There is no open sign-up.
  2. You complete your profile and upload your evidence.
  3. A member of Kiam staff reviews the profile copy, and a verifier checks the documents. Evidence is stored separately from anything public, and every time a member of staff opens one of your documents it is logged.
  4. The listing goes live once the required checks are verified.
  5. You can edit your listing, and take it down yourself at any time, without asking anyone.

Keeping a listing

Insurance and DBS certificates expire, and the verification badge expires with them, automatically. You will be reminded before that happens. Registrations are re-checked against the public registers quarterly.
