What we aim for

We build this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2 at level AA. A full independent audit is scheduled before the directory launches, and this page will state the outcome when it is done. Until then, treat this as a description of what the site does rather than as a certified conformance claim.

Display and reading settings

Every page has an accessibility panel in the header. It changes text size, turns on a higher-contrast palette, turns off motion and switches to a typeface some people with dyslexia find easier. Your choice is remembered and applied before the page is drawn, so nothing flashes at you on the way in.

What we have deliberately not done

  • No carousels and no auto-advancing content. Nothing on this site moves unless you ask it to.
  • No time limits. No form expires while you are filling it in, and no page pushes you along.
  • No CAPTCHAs. Puzzles at the door are a real barrier; we use methods that ask nothing of you instead.
  • No scrolling banner at the top of every page. Persistent moving text is exhausting, and this site has nothing urgent enough to justify it.

Keyboard and screen readers

  • Everything works with a keyboard alone. Focus is always visible.
  • A “skip to content” link is the first thing on every page.
  • Headings run in order, form fields have real labels, and errors are described in plain language next to the field they belong to.
  • When you reveal a contact detail on a listing, your keyboard focus moves to it and the detail is announced.

Motion

If your device is set to reduce motion, we respect it — animations and transitions are switched off rather than shortened. You can also switch them off from the accessibility panel regardless of your device settings.

Where we know we fall short

  • The independent WCAG 2.2 AA audit has not yet been carried out, so this page cannot yet claim a conformance level.
  • Practitioners write their own profile text. We check it for a number of things, but we cannot guarantee every listing is written in plain language.

Telling us about a problem

If something on this site is hard or impossible to use, please email {{ kiam_ui.CONTACT.email|default:'enquiries@kiamclinic.com' }} and say what you were trying to do. You do not need to know the technical term for what went wrong. We would rather have a vague report than none.
