What the badge says

A listing showing “Credentials checked” and a date means that, on that date, Kiam Clinic had seen and checked the documents listed below, and that none of them had expired. The date shown is the oldest of those checks, so the badge is only ever as fresh as its weakest element.

What the badge does not say

It is a documents check. It is not a judgement about anybody's practice.

  • It is not a quality, competence or outcome claim. Kiam has not assessed how good a practitioner is, and could not — it neither provides nor supervises their care.
  • It is not a recommendation or an endorsement. Nobody is listed here because Kiam thinks they are the right person for you.
  • It does not mean Kiam is responsible for the care you receive. Practitioners listed here are independent and are responsible for their own practice and their own regulation.
  • It says nothing about anything Kiam has not been shown. Checks are made against documents and public registers, not against a practitioner's clinical records.

The badge lapses on its own

Verification is worked out from the dates on those documents, every night. Nobody at Kiam can switch a badge on, and nobody has to remember to switch one off: if an insurance certificate expires, the badge goes on the day it expires, without anyone doing anything.

Work with children and young people

A practitioner is only listed as seeing children or young people once an enhanced DBS certificate has been verified. Where that check has not been completed, the listing may still appear — but only for adult work. Its under-18 client groups are hidden from the page and excluded from search results until the certificate has been checked.

If you are looking for someone to see a child or a young person, check that their listing names the age group you need. If it does not, ask them directly before booking.

Registrations are re-checked

Regulators publish their registers, and Kiam re-checks the practitioners listed here against them quarterly. A listing does not stay up on the strength of a check made once, at the start.

If you think a listing is wrong

Report a concern about a listing. A concern about a practitioner's registration is the one we act on fastest. A complaint about the care you received goes to their regulator instead — that page names them.
