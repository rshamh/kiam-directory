Practitioners listed here are independent professionals. Kiam Clinic does not employ or supervise them and cannot investigate the care they provided. A complaint about someone's care, safety or conduct goes to the body that regulates them — not to Kiam.

Start with the practitioner themselves if you feel able to; every practitioner is required to have a complaints procedure. Otherwise, contact their regulator:

  • Doctors, including psychiatrists — the General Medical Council (GMC).
  • Practitioner psychologists, occupational therapists, arts therapists, dietitians and speech and language therapists — the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC).
  • Nurses — the Nursing and Midwifery Council (NMC).
  • Pharmacists — the General Pharmaceutical Council (GPhC).
  • Social workers — Social Work England.

Counsellors and psychotherapists are not regulated by law, but the great majority belong to a professional body that holds a register and takes complaints — the BACP, UKCP, BABCP, BPC, NCPS, BPS, ACP or AFT. The listing shows which body a practitioner is registered with; that body's website has its complaints procedure.

If the care was provided by a service registered with the Care Quality Commission, you can tell the CQC as well.
