What this directory is

The Kiam Clinic Directory lists independent mental-health practitioners working in the UK — psychiatrists, psychologists, psychotherapists, counsellors and allied professionals — with their professions, specialities, approaches, languages, fees and locations. You can read a listing, decide whether someone looks right for you, and contact them directly.

What Kiam Clinic does

  • Publishes the listings, and checks the evidence behind them.
  • Checks each practitioner's identity, professional registration, qualifications and indemnity insurance, and re-checks registrations while a listing stays up. How verification works.
  • Takes listings down when something is wrong with them.

What Kiam Clinic does not do

  • It does not employ or supervise the practitioners listed here. They are independent professionals responsible for their own practice, their own clinical decisions and their own regulation.
  • It does not arrange appointments and holds no booking on your behalf. You arrange your appointment with the practitioner.
  • It does not take payment. Fees are set by the practitioner and paid to them directly. Kiam takes no commission on anyone's fees.
  • It does not choose a practitioner for you and does not match or triage. What you see is what matches the filters you set.
  • It does not provide care through this directory, and it is not an emergency or crisis service.

How practitioners get listed

Listing is by invitation and membership. A practitioner submits their details and their evidence, a member of Kiam staff reviews both, and the listing goes live only once the required checks are verified. Practitioners pay a membership fee to be listed; that fee buys a listing, and nothing about it changes what the verification checks require. More for practitioners.

If something is wrong with a listing

Tell us — report a concern about a listing. If your concern is about the care a practitioner provided, that goes to their regulator rather than to Kiam, and that page names them.

Kiam Clinic

Kiam Clinic is a private mental-health clinic in Epsom, Surrey, and is the publisher of this directory. Its own clinical services are a separate thing from the practitioners listed here — see kiamclinic.com.
