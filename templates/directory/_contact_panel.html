{% comment %} ContactRevealPanel — the contact block on a profile. Contact details are NOT in this markup. Each channel renders a button that posts to /p//contact//, and the detail arrives in the response. That is the whole protection: a scraper pulling profile HTML finds no addresses, because there are none to find. Progressive enhancement, in this order: * Without JavaScript the form posts normally and the reveal is a whole page. This is not a courtesy — it is the difference between "contactable" and "not" for anyone whose script blocking, corporate proxy or flaky connection ate the bundle. * With HTMX the response replaces the button in place, inside `.dir-contact__slot`. Focus, not a live region. `hx-on::after-swap` moves focus into the revealed panel, which is a labelled `role="group"` with `tabindex="-1"`. An `aria-live` region on the slot would work too, but combining the two makes a screen reader read the independence notice and the address twice over — on a site whose audience has a high rate of anxiety and neurodevelopmental conditions, redundant chatter is a cost, not a safety net. Moving focus to new content is the WCAG-recommended pattern and it also puts the keyboard where the visitor wants it: on the address. Focusing a group announces its NAME, not its contents, so the panel is labelled by its heading *and* its value — see _contact_revealed.html. That way the focus move itself says "Email address, jane@example.com" and the reveal announces the thing that was revealed, with nothing read twice. `hx-on::before-swap` is load-bearing, not defensive. htmx 2's default `responseHandling` does not swap 4xx at all, so the rate-limit refusal was being fetched, discarded, and never shown: the button just sat there and the click did nothing. Opting 429 in explicitly, here rather than by editing `htmx.config` globally, keeps the honest status code on the response. TODO(sign-off): CQC compliance lead — "You contact directly. Kiam Clinic does not pass on messages, arrange appointments, or take payment." That sentence is the independence relationship stated at the moment somebody acts on it. `booking_url` is deliberately NOT rendered here. A "book an appointment" control on a Kiam-branded page asserts that Kiam manages the appointment, which is one of the three things in docs/content-compliance.md §9 that change Kiam's regulatory position. Raise it for legal review before adding it. {% endcomment %}
