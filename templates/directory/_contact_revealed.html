{% comment %} The revealed contact detail, and the interstitial that comes with it. Params: practitioner · display_name · channel · value · href · heading_level (3 in the profile's Contact section, 2 on the standalone page). **The independence notice is above the detail, not beside it and not after it.** docs/content-compliance.md §5 requires it verbatim in the contact reveal, and DOM order is the only ordering a screen reader has: someone landing here with focus reads the relationship before they read the address. Rendered from the one shared partial — the sentence exists in exactly one place in this repo. `tabindex="-1"` and `role="group"` make this a focus destination with an accessible name. HTMX moves focus here after the swap (see _contact_panel.html), so the reveal announces itself without a live region shouting over it. The group is labelled by its heading AND its value, and that is the whole announcement mechanism. Focusing a container announces its accessible name and role — it does NOT read the descendants; the user has to arrow down for those. So `aria-labelledby="…-heading …-value"` makes the focus move itself say "Email address, jane@example.com, group": the fact and the content, once each, with nothing duplicated. Labelling it by the heading alone would announce "Email address, group" and leave the address unread until the user went looking. The website link carries rel="sponsored nofollow noopener": listings are a paid membership, and passing PageRank from a paid placement is a link scheme however well meant. `sponsored` is the token Google's current guidance asks for; `nofollow` stays for crawlers that predate it. {% endcomment %}
