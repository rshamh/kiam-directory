This subdomain's OWN robots.txt. Never shared with kiamclinic.com or
rooms.kiamclinic.com — search engines treat subdomains as separate sites
(docs/seo.md, docs/multi-project-architecture.md §5).

The disallow list is the account area and the staff console. Faceted search URLs
are NOT disallowed here: they must stay crawlable so their `noindex, follow` and
their canonical to /search/ are actually read. Blocking them in robots.txt would
stop the crawler ever seeing either, and Google would keep the URLs in the index
with no idea what they are.

`/search/places/` IS disallowed, and the distinction is the point. It returns
head-less HTML — bare <option> elements for the location field's datalist — so it
cannot carry a `noindex` meta tag, and `?near=` is unbounded, so it would be one
thin URL per typed prefix. It also fronts an outbound call to postcodes.io, so a
crawler walking prefixes would drive somebody else's rate limit. The view sets an
`X-Robots-Tag` header too; this is the layer that stops the fetch happening at all.
