A card-sized anchor wrapping a headshot, three pills and a badge gives a screen reader one enormous link whose accessible name is the entire card read aloud, and it makes the pills untargetable. One clear link, named after the person, is what somebody navigating by links needs to hear. **Featured listings are labelled here, at the point of display.** Nothing is featured yet; the label exists before the first person pays for one, because undisclosed paid ranking breaches CAP rules (docs/content-compliance.md §6) and a label added later is a label somebody has to remember to add. Contact details are deliberately absent, exactly as on the profile. The card links to the profile and the reveal happens there. {% endcomment %}
