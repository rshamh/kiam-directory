It is NOT a competence, quality or outcome claim, and the copy must never drift towards one — no "approved", no "trusted", no "recommended", no tick that reads as an endorsement. The link to /how-verification-works/ is part of the badge rather than an optional extra, because the policy requires the limits of the claim to be one click away from wherever the claim appears. Absence is meaningful: a published listing whose insurance lapsed loses the badge automatically overnight and renders nothing here. There is deliberately no "not verified" state — a badge nobody can set by hand also should not be a label that shames the listing it is missing from. `practitioner.is_verified` and `credentials_checked_at` are COMPUTED (directory/services/verification.py).
