{% extends "base.html" %} {% comment %} /search/ — the whole page on a normal GET, the #results fragment when HTMX asks. **ONE form wraps the search bar, the sidebar and the results.** Not three forms, and not inputs associated by the `form=` attribute: with JavaScript off, a visitor who has typed a location and then ticks "accepting new clients" must submit BOTH, and separate forms would silently drop whichever one they did not press. The layout is CSS grid inside a single form, so the markup order is the reading order and nothing depends on script to hold the state together. `#results` is inside that form on purpose too. It contains no inputs — the pagination links are real anchors — so the form serialises exactly the filters, and HTMX can swap the container without touching them. The result count lives OUTSIDE #results, and the fragment updates it with an out-of-band `innerHTML` swap. A live region that is itself replaced by the swap is not reliably announced; one that persists and has its contents changed is. How these results are ordered

Results are ordered by how well they match what you asked for. The things that count, most first: how close the practitioner is to the location you gave, how well their listing matches your words, how complete their listing is, whether their credentials have been checked and are in date, whether they are accepting new clients, and how recently they updated their listing.

Practitioners with equally good matches are shuffled, and the shuffle changes daily, so no one listing sits at the top permanently.

Practitioners can pay for a featured place near the top. Featured listings are always labelled “Paid placement” where they appear, and no more than {{ featured_cap }} can appear on a page. Paying changes position only — it does not change whether somebody is listed, and it has no effect at all on the verification checks.

Kiam Clinic does not rank practitioners by quality and does not recommend one over another. What the verification badge means.
