{% comment %} Sign-in form. ONE form, one button, two routes: fill the password box and it is a password sign-in, leave it blank and we email a link. Two tabs would be worse. Someone who has never set a password would have to know that in order to pick the right tab, and someone who has would have to hunt for it — this way the page works without the visitor knowing which kind of account they have. Non-field errors are in a role="alert" so a failed attempt or a rate-limit refusal is announced rather than only appearing (WCAG 4.1.3). The honeypot is .sr-only rather than display:none — a display:none field is skipped by some bots, which is exactly backwards. {% endcomment %}
